About Me

Hello! My name is Zahra Hashim Mohamed, and I specialize in GIS and spatial analysis. I have a passion for creating visually compelling maps and leveraging spatial data to solve real-world problems. My work focuses on integrating creativity with technical precision to generate meaningful geospatial insights.

With expertise in ArcGIS, QGIS, SQL, and spatial data visualization, I bring a detail-oriented approach to analyzing geographic patterns and trends. I am always eager to explore innovative solutions in mapping, data integration, and urban spatial analysis.
